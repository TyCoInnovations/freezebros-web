Privacy policy

We only collect what we need to get your treats to you, and we never sell your details.

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • your name, email address and phone number (if you provide it)
  • your shipping and billing address
  • the details of your order

How we use it

  • to process, pack and deliver your order
  • to send you order updates and answer your questions
  • to meet our legal and tax obligations

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Freeze Bros never sees or stores your full card details. You can read how Stripe handles your data in Stripe’s privacy policy.

Cookies and storage

We save the contents of your cart in your browser so it’s still there when you come back. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Sharing your information

We only share your details with the services needed to fulfil your order, such as our payment provider and delivery partners. We never sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. Email us at hello@freezebros.com.au.
