Why freeze dried?

Same treats. Totally new crunch.

  • Crunchy, not chewy

    Freeze drying turns soft treats light and crispy. Every bite cracks, then melts in your mouth.

  • Big, bold flavour

    Taking the water out leaves all the flavour in. It’s the treat you know, turned right up.

  • Stays fresh for ages

    Sealed packs keep for months. Perfect for the pantry, the lunchbox or a long road trip.
